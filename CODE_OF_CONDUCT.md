# Code of conduct

Quicx is built in the open, and everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent, including when disagreeing;
- keep criticism about the work, never about the person;
- welcome questions from newcomers;
- not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone for any reason.

Comments or contributions that break these rules may be edited or removed, and repeated or serious cases can lead to a ban from the project. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [Dimitar Anastasov](https://github.com/anastassow), through GitHub.
